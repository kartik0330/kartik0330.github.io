## Overview

From Jun to Nov 2025 at Liquidnitro Games I worked on network-synced multiplayer mini-games in Unity. Details of unreleased titles are under NDA, so this page covers the engineering only.

## Focus areas

- **Netcode:** gameplay state, player input and environment interactions synced across clients with Photon Fusion.
- **Procedural paths:** a path generation system that builds playable routes at runtime.
- **Obstacles and AI:** hit detection and avoidance that adapts to live gameplay conditions, shared by players and AI agents.
- **Performance:** profiling and reducing latency of multiplayer interactions together with design and art.
